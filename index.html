A Rural Rule

Put the Homestead in order.

A modern farm ledger for ordering the daily work of the homestead—keeping its records, tending what has been entrusted to your care, and giving shape to the rhythm of the day.

Inspired by the ordered rhythm of the Benedictine Rule and way of life, where prayer, work, responsibility, and rest each have their place, Regula Rustica—the rural rule—helps bring that same sense of order to your daily stewardship.

App roles control what an invited account may do. Household-only people do not need a role.

What do these roles mean?
Steward
Full Homestead administration and data authority.
Keeper
Trusted day-to-day management of records, tasks, Journal, notes, ledger, and photos.
Hand
Limited participation in ordinary work, assigned tasks, approved events, and observations.
Guest
Read-only access.

Everyone listed here may share in the work. Only those you invite need a Regula Rustica account. Invitations remain optional and may be sent later.

The Rule in Practice

Your day now has an order.

The Homestead has its name, the household is known, what is entrusted to your care has been recorded, and the recurring work has found its place. This is how Regula Rustica begins: not with more administration, but with a clearer rhythm for the work already before you.
